## Business Value
Mobbin provides instant access to a vast library of real-world UI/UX design patterns from top-tier applications. It accelerates the design and development process by allowing Y-OS to reference proven app flows and visual structures directly through natural language.
## Capabilities
- Search UI/UX designs using natural language queries.
- Retrieve reference screen images of proven app patterns and flows.
- Browse and filter designs by categories, UI elements, and platforms.
## Dependencies
- Active Mobbin paid subscription.
- OAuth authentication configured in Y-OS.
## Known Limits & Bugs
- Image retrieval may be limited by API rate limits or subscription tier.
- Search categories may require exact casing for optimal results.
## Workarounds & Lessons
- Ensure search queries are specific to yield the most relevant UI patterns.
- For URL lookups, ensure the full product page URL is passed as a string.
