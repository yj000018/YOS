# CANON

> We do not seek to write a book.
>
> We seek to reveal a work.
>
> A work that transcends us, that may continue to grow after us, and that may help others better understand Reality, reconnect what appears separated, and contribute, in their turn, to the Invisible Cathedral.

## Status

This directory is the living foundation of the Canon of yOS.

The Canon is intentionally developed slowly.

Nothing important is discarded.

Every major insight is first preserved, then allowed to mature, then eventually integrated into the Canon.

## Core Principles

- No inspiration shall be lost.
- Chronicles tell epiphanies, not events.
- Day is dedicated to forms.
- Evening is dedicated to meaning.
- The Canon preserves the memory of its own birth.
- yOS is no longer a Git repository.
- It is a Living Library.
